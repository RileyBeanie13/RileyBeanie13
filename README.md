# Hi, I'm Riley!

## About Me

I'm a cybersecurity student who switched over from aerospace engineering in late 2025. I spent my first few months on TryHackMe getting a taste of what cybersecurity was like, and from there I started focusing on the blue team side. I began with security operations on LetsDefend, investigating alerts in a simulated SOC, and that's what led to my blue team projects, which are where I do most of my exploring now. Most of that has been in detection engineering, learning to write rules that catch threats, and I've also dabbled in AI security, learning both how to integrate AI into existing security tools and how to defend it against prompt injection.

That's where I'm at for now, and from here on, I'm onto the next thing! 

## What I've Worked On

**[detection-engineering-lab](https://github.com/RileyBeanie13/detection-engineering-lab)** (Aug 17 – Oct 15, 2026): a Wazuh home lab with six custom detection rules, each written for a gap in the default ruleset after running the attack it detects and documented in its own finding. On top of the rules, I built an LLM-assisted triage layer that sends every alert to a locally hosted Qwen model, writes its verdict back into the Wazuh dashboard, and was tested against prompt injection.

**[letsdefend-writeups](https://github.com/RileyBeanie13/letsdefend-writeups)** (Jul 26 – Aug 15, 2026): my investigation writeups for LetsDefend SOC and monitoring alerts, focused on explaining the evidence behind each verdict rather than just the verdict itself.
